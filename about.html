About us

A Hong Kong firm where technology meets capital

Galaxy Investment Limited was founded on a simple observation: the companies that win in Asia need engineering excellence and investment discipline in equal measure — and rarely find both in one place.

Our story

From an office in Central, Hong Kong, we began by helping technology founders ship products that could stand up to enterprise scrutiny. As those clients grew, they asked us harder questions — about market entry, about fundraising, about governance.

So we built a firm that could answer them. Today Galaxy brings together engineers, strategists, and investment professionals who have operated across Greater China, ASEAN, and beyond.

We remain deliberately independent and deliberately hands-on. When we take on a mandate, we stay until the result is real.

Mission

To be the most trusted partner for ambitious businesses building and investing across Asia — pairing world-class technology with disciplined capital.

Vision

A region where great ideas are never held back by the gap between what to build and how to fund it.

Built for the long horizon
